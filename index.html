Lahore

Lahore is the capital and largest city of the Pakistani province of Punjab, and the second-largest city in the country with a population of over 14 million residents. Known as "The Heart of Pakistan," it is a major economic, educational, and cultural hub with a rich history spanning over a millennium.
Key Highlights
• Geography & Administration: Located on the Ravi River, the district spans across 10 tehsils including Model Town, Lahore Cantt, and Raiwind, with an area of roughly 1,772 km² (684 sq mi).
• History & Heritage: Ruled across centuries by the Ghaznavids, the Mughal Empire, the Sikh Empire, and the British Raj, Lahore features iconic architecture such as the Lahore Fort (Shahi Qila), Badshahi Mosque, Shalimar Gardens, and Wazir Khan Mosque.
• Culture & Food: Recognized as a UNESCO City of Literature, Lahore is celebrated for its deep-rooted culinary traditions, bustling bazaars like Anarkali, and historic sites within the Walled City of Lahore Authority.
• Transportation: The city features modern urban transit systems including the Orange Line (Pakistan's first overhead metro train)
, the Metrobus, and the Speedo bus network
